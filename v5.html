Three Portable Espresso Makers for Travel, Compared (2026)

TrailPress Mini. A compact lever press, 640 g, makes a single shot at a time. Simple to clean; the lever needs a firm surface.

Voyager Brew Cup. A steep-and-press cup, 480 g, forgiving for beginners. Produces a milder cup than a true espresso.

AuroraPress Go. A hand-pump espresso maker, 720 g, with a metal filter basket. Heavier than the other two; the pump handle doubles as a tamper.

All three fit in a carry-on side pocket. Choice depends on whether you value weight, simplicity, or a metal basket.
